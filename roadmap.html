To Do Planned, unfinished, and future development work

Attack Command

Allow Player combat units to receive a direct order to attack a selected hostile target.

Attack-Move Command

Allow combat vehicles to move toward a destination while engaging hostile targets encountered along the way.

Combat Effects

Add muzzle flashes, impact effects, explosions, and vehicle or building destruction effects.

Enemy Building Prefabs

Continue creating and configuring enemy-owned buildings using the current faction, health, selection, and destruction systems.

RTS-Style Enemy Base and Economy AI

Replace free enemy spawning with an AI-controlled faction that follows the same rules as the Player. The Enemy should collect and store resources, pay construction and production costs, build its own base, produce vehicles through normal production queues, form attack groups, replace losses, and rebuild destroyed infrastructure.

Faction-Specific Economies

Separate Player and Enemy Money, physical resources, storage, and manpower so each faction has an independent economy that can support construction, production, and future logistics AI.

Enemy Logistics Routes

Allow Enemy logistics vehicles to use the same resource nodes, cargo, warehouses, and persistent route systems as the Player so disrupting enemy transportation directly affects its economy.

Enemy Attack Groups

Have the Enemy assemble actually produced combat vehicles into attack groups, launch attacks when force requirements are met, and then rebuild the next force instead of spawning attackers for free.

Fog of War and Vision

Add Hidden, Explored, and Visible map states with configurable sight ranges. Combat systems should require current visibility before acquiring or firing on hostile targets.

Minimap

Add a minimap showing terrain, Player units, buildings, resources, and enemy contacts.

Enable NavMesh for Built Roads

Update runtime navigation so Player-built roads, bridges, and other traversable surfaces can affect NavMesh movement.

Road Navigation Preference

Add a dedicated Road NavMesh Area so vehicles prefer roads when calculating paths.

Construction Sites

Replace completed-building construction with temporary construction sites that remain until construction requirements are met.

Construction Material Delivery

Require physical resources to be delivered to construction sites before buildings can be completed.

Automatic Material Requirements

Allow logistics vehicles to deliver only the amount of each resource still required by a construction site.

Construction Labor

Allow nearby vehicles to contribute construction progress, with dedicated construction vehicles providing a speed bonus.

Import / Export System

Expand the resource economy with Import and Export Warehouses connected to Shipping and Receiving.

Seven-Day Logistics Cycle

Process Headquarters orders and exports on a repeating seven-game-day schedule using the existing game-time system.

Building Operating Costs

Give selected buildings recurring monetary operating expenses in addition to their initial construction costs, making base expansion increase ongoing financial requirements.

Building Income and Expense Tracking

Track money generated and consumed by buildings and display operating costs, revenue, and potentially net profitability in the UI.

Raw Resource Economy

Expand resource collection around raw materials that must be physically harvested and transported before they can be used or processed.

Resource Refining

Add factories that convert raw materials into refined resources such as Fuel, processed Metal, purified Water, and other usable industrial materials.

Advanced Manufacturing

Add additional production stages that combine refined materials into finished goods such as Ammunition, vehicle parts, Electronics, explosives, and construction materials.

Automated Production Chains

Allow logistics routes, warehouses, factories, and vehicles to form automated supply chains that continuously collect, transport, process, and distribute resources.

Export Economy

Allow surplus resources and manufactured products to be transported to Export Warehouses and sold for Money.

Production Profitability Information

Display production value, operating expenses, and estimated profit for industrial buildings or production chains where appropriate.

Building Pause / Shutdown Controls

Allow production facilities to be temporarily paused to stop production and reduce or eliminate operating expenses when they are not needed.

Ammunition Resource

Add Ammunition as a physical manufactured resource required by combat units rather than allowing weapons to fire indefinitely.

Ammunition Manufacturing

Add factories that manufacture Ammunition from appropriate processed resources.

Ammunition Distribution

Require Ammunition to be transported from factories or warehouses to military facilities, forward bases, or combat units.

Weapon-Specific Ammunition Consumption

Make different weapon systems consume Ammunition at different rates, with artillery and rocket systems requiring substantially more logistical support than lighter weapons.

Food Supply System

Make Food an operational resource used to support manpower, facilities, or other military operations.

Water Supply System

Make Water an operational resource used to support manpower and base operations.

Fuel Consumption System

Expand Fuel into an actively consumed logistical resource for vehicles, generators, and selected buildings.

Forward Supply Points

Allow Ammo, Fuel, Food, Water, and other supplies to be staged closer to combat areas so units do not always depend directly on the main base.

Electricity System

Add electrical power as a support system for industrial and advanced military facilities.

Mobile Generator Trailer

Add a towable generator that consumes Fuel and provides electrical support to nearby buildings.

Mobile Generator Vehicle

Add a generator mounted on a truck or flatbed platform that can move with forward bases and provide power within a configurable radius.

Generator Fuel Consumption

Make generators continuously consume Fuel while operating, creating another logistics requirement for powered facilities.

Power Radius System

Provide electrical power to buildings within a generator's effective area without requiring a detailed cable or electrical grid simulation.

Powered Production Bonus

Give powered factories and production facilities increased production speed or efficiency.

Power-Required Advanced Buildings

Allow selected advanced facilities to require electricity to operate while basic structures remain functional without power.

Permanent Base Generator

Add a larger stationary generator building with greater output and coverage than mobile generator systems.

Power Infrastructure as Strategic Targets

Make generators and other power infrastructure meaningful strategic targets whose destruction can reduce production or disable advanced systems.

Integrated Logistics and Industrial Economy

Create a complete economic loop where raw resources are harvested, transported, refined, manufactured into useful or valuable products, distributed to military forces or exported for Money, and used to fund continuing military and industrial operations.

Resource Tooltip Interaction

Allow individual warehouse and vehicle entries in resource tooltips to select or focus the camera on that object.

Production Queue Visuals

Replace the simple queue count with visual queue boxes or unit icons showing upcoming production.

Finish Command Hover Tooltips

Finish the command-button tooltip system by making tooltips follow the mouse and adding tooltip information for Build and Back navigation buttons while retaining live name, description, resource-cost, and manpower information for construction and production options.

Resource and Manpower Warning Popups

Finish world-positioned warning messages for failed construction and production attempts. Building shortages should appear near the attempted construction location, while production and manpower shortages should appear above the selected production building.

Game Speed Controls

Add HUD controls for pause, normal speed, and accelerated game speeds.

UI Scaling and Layout

Continue refining HUD scaling, anchors, panel sizing, and support for different screen resolutions.

More Vehicles and Buildings

Continue expanding the military and logistics vehicle roster and adding additional functional buildings.

Asset Updates

Continue replacing primitive and placeholder geometry with low-poly vehicle, building, and environment assets.

Upgrades System

Add building and unit upgrades using progression levels such as Level I, II, and III.

Helicopters

Add aircraft using a separate movement system that does not rely on the ground NavMesh.

Additional Combat Units

Add more armored vehicles and other military combat unit types.

Main Battle Tank

Add a dedicated heavily armored direct-fire combat vehicle with strong frontline capability, turret rotation, high health, and configurable Ammunition use.

Self-Propelled Artillery

Add a dedicated indirect-fire artillery vehicle separate from the tank role, providing long-range fire support with slower firing, limited close-range capability, and high Ammunition consumption.

M142 HIMARS-Style Rocket Artillery

Add a fast wheeled rocket artillery vehicle capable of long-range salvo attacks, balanced by significant Ammunition resupply requirements.

M270 MLRS-Style Rocket Artillery

Add a heavier tracked multiple-launch rocket system with larger salvo capacity at the cost of mobility and greater logistics requirements.

Recon Drones

Add deployable drones for scouting, spotting enemies, extending vision, and supporting long-range weapons.

Drone Upgrades and Variants

Allow drones to receive specialized upgrades or roles, including improved reconnaissance, weapons, and one-way explosive contact-detonation configurations.

8×8 Heavy Logistics Platform

Add a heavy wheeled logistics platform based around PLS and wrecker-style vehicles that can support multiple specialized configurations.

8×8 Cargo Truck

Add a high-capacity cargo configuration for transporting bulk physical resources between resource sites, warehouses, factories, and bases.

8×8 Fuel Truck

Add a dedicated high-capacity Fuel transport configuration for supplying vehicles, generators, and facilities.

8×8 Repair / Wrecker Vehicle

Add a mobile repair and recovery vehicle capable of servicing damaged vehicles in the field.

MRAP / Protected Scout Vehicle

Add mine-resistant vehicles for protected reconnaissance, convoy escort, route scouting, and operating in hazardous areas.

Route-Clearance Vehicle

Add a specialized protected vehicle capable of detecting and clearing mines along important logistics routes.

Mine Deployment System

Allow selected engineer or support vehicles to deploy mines, potentially as an upgrade or equipment option instead of a completely separate vehicle.

Air-Defense Vehicle

Add a mobile air-defense unit designed to protect bases, logistics formations, and artillery from drones and future aircraft.

Forward Observer / Recon Vehicle

Add a lightly armed reconnaissance vehicle designed to locate targets and provide targeting information for artillery and rocket systems.

Transport / Attack Helicopters

Expand helicopter roles to include transport, reconnaissance, logistics, and combat using movement independent from the ground NavMesh.

Additional Map

Create another playable map and continue work toward supporting custom maps or heightmap imports.

Map Development

Continue expanding terrain detail with roads, trees, hills, bridges, resource areas, bases, and strategic routes.

Finish Menu Functions and Level Launcher

Expand the current in-game menu framework with functional Controls, Settings, and Exit Game actions, then add a front-end level launcher for starting the game and selecting available maps or levels.

Custom Cursor

Replace the default system cursor with a game-specific cursor and eventually support different cursor states for movement, attacking, selection, construction, and other RTS commands.

Music and Sound Effects

Add background music and gameplay audio for vehicles, weapons, construction, destruction, UI interactions, alerts, and other game events.

Completed Systems that are currently implemented and working

Battle Tank

A tracked direct-fire combat vehicle is implemented with selectable movement, routing, health, turret rotation, and aim-before-fire behavior.

Tracked Vehicle Production

A dedicated production building can manufacture tracked combat vehicles using the reusable production queue.

HIMARS

A mobile long-range rocket artillery vehicle is implemented with launcher rotation, multi-rocket salvos, splash damage, impact effects, and configurable salvo spread.

Tracked Artillery

A tracked indirect-fire vehicle is implemented with a single high-damage projectile and large splash radius.

Long-Range Projectile Combat

Reusable projectile attack and projectile-flight systems support arcing trajectories, launcher aiming, minimum range, salvo timing, spread, splash damage, and impact effects.

Building Construction Costs

Building prefabs expose configurable construction costs and placement checks affordability before accepting an order.

Construction Cancellation Refunds

Unfinished building orders retain a cost receipt so canceled, replaced, queued, or interrupted construction can return the resources that were spent.

Construction Placement States

Building placement uses transparent grey for valid previews, transparent red for invalid locations, and transparent light orange for confirmed construction ghosts.

Developer Commands Panel

An independently toggleable developer panel includes a resource cheat that adds Money and fills Player-owned warehouses for testing.

In-Game Menu Framework

An in-game menu provides Return to Game, Controls, Settings, Dev Commands, and Exit Game buttons, with Return to Game and Dev Commands currently functional.

HQ Focus Faction Filter

Headquarters quick access now explicitly searches for the Player-owned HQ so the Enemy Headquarters cannot be selected by the shortcut.

Command Tooltip Foundation

Command buttons can expose names, descriptions, building or production costs, and manpower information using the same data that drives gameplay.

RTS Camera

Camera movement supports arrow keys, edge scrolling, zooming, and focusing on selected objects.

NavMesh Vehicle Movement

Ground vehicles use Unity NavMesh navigation for movement across the RTS map.

Unit Selection

Selectable objects support individual click selection, drag-selection, and visible selection indicators.

Dynamic Command Panel

A reusable 3×4 QWER / ASDF / ZXCV command panel displays commands based on the selected Player object.

Faction and Ownership System

Selectable objects can belong to the Player, Enemy, or Neutral faction.

Enemy and Neutral Selection

Enemy and Neutral objects can be selected and inspected without allowing the Player to control them.

Building Placement

Buildings use grid-based placement, ghost previews, placement validation, and vehicle-based construction orders.

Production Queue

Production buildings can queue units using configurable build times, resource costs, and manpower requirements.

Production Rally Points

Newly produced vehicles can automatically move toward a configured rally point.

Vehicle Cargo System

Vehicles can physically carry configurable amounts and types of physical resources.

Resource Nodes

Physical resource nodes provide resources for logistics vehicles to collect.

Specialized Warehouses

Warehouses store individual physical resource types with configurable capacities.

Warehouse Shipping and Receiving

Logistics vehicles can load and unload resources through warehouse transfer points.

Persistent Logistics Routes

Vehicles can follow reusable logistics routes containing road waypoints, resource nodes, and warehouse transfer points.

Resource Economy

The current economy includes Money, Food, Water, Fuel, Wood, Metal, and Electronics.

Resource Top Bar

The HUD displays stored and in-transit physical resources along with global Money.

Resource Hover Breakdown

Resource tooltips show which warehouses and vehicles currently contain each resource.

Manpower System

Units consume manpower capacity when produced and return their manpower when destroyed.

Barracks Manpower Capacity

Barracks increase the manpower cap and remove that capacity when destroyed.

Over-Cap Manpower Handling

The manpower cap can fall below current usage without destroying units, while additional production remains blocked.

Selected Object Information Panel

Vehicles, buildings, resource nodes, health, cargo, and production information can be displayed in the bottom HUD.

World Health Bars

Damaged vehicles and buildings show green remaining health over a red missing-health background.

Selected Health Bar

Selected objects use the same green and red health display in the SelectedInfoPanel.

Production Progress Bar

Production progress is displayed using a reusable Simple UISprite-based progress bar.

Basic Vehicle Combat

Armed vehicles automatically detect hostile objects, rotate toward targets, fire, deal damage, and destroy targets.

Combat Targeting of Buildings

Combat vehicles can attack hostile buildings without requiring those buildings to have their own CombatUnit.

Weapon Tracers

Weapon fire displays a visible yellow LineRenderer tracer between the weapon and target.

Enemy Attack Routes

Enemy vehicles follow predefined waypoint routes using NavMesh movement.

Enemy Vehicle Spawning

Enemy vehicles can be spawned automatically at configurable intervals and assigned to an attack route.

Destructible Enemy Production Building

Enemy spawning is attached to a destructible building, and destroying the structure stops future enemy production.

Destruction Resource Cleanup

Destroyed warehouses and cargo vehicles are automatically removed from stored and in-transit resource totals.

HQ Quick Access

A HUD button selects the Headquarters and centers the camera on it when clicked again.

Game Time System

The game tracks day, hour, and minute values and supports pause and adjustable time speed.

Development Website

The project website contains a homepage, combined changelog and documentation page, project overview, and roadmap.
